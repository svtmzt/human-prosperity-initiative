## 2. Founder choice: record one of three options only when actually decided

The options below are **decision alternatives, not a resolution**. The founder decision is **PENDING**, and the working operating mode remains public-source desk research only.

| Proposed path | What it changes after a real decision | Specific missing proof / stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HOLD** — maintain an unfunded, unincorporated open-source desk programme | Continue citable public research, tooling, corrections and non-sensitive peer critique without claiming institutional authorisation | No grant contracts, charity numbers, formal trustees, sensitive intake or field study; assess individual contractual/liability risks before making commitments |
| **FISCAL HOST** — explore a qualified charity/university/research host | A real eligible host could contract/administer clearly scoped funds/research only under its own authorised procedures and written agreement | Verify actual host interest, eligibility, charitable objects, grants, accountable signing/data/ethics roles, overhead, IP, conflicts, exit and independent science; **no host exists yet** |
| **INCORPORATE** — conditional England/Wales charity route | A suitably justified Foundation/Association CIO or charitable company could acquire the legal capacity of a newly registered entity | Obtain charity-law advice on exclusive charitable/public-benefit purposes, suitable trustees, constitution, registerable name, costs, reporting obligations and approved application; **nothing filed or registered** |

The earlier F0-04.2 desk pack provisionally favours **HOLD while assessing a host**, with a **Foundation CIO only as a conditional later possibility**. This is prior *draft advice*, not a new founder decision and not a commitment to that legal form. A CIC would be a separate later commercial/social-enterprise consideration, not a charity-registration shortcut.

**Current official reference checks (16 November 2026):**
- [Charity Commission/GOV.UK set-up sequence](https://www.gov.uk/setting-up-charity/set-up-a-charity): actual trustees, public-benefit purposes, structure, governing document and applicable registration are substantive requirements.
- [CIO model constitutions](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/setting-up-a-charity-model-governing-documents): Foundation has trustee-only voting membership; Association has wider voting members; a draft paragraph is not a filed model constitution.
- [Registration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/setting-up-charity/register-your-charity): real trustee, governance, name, address and financial information must be supplied where applicable. Do not create a dummy application or infer eligibility.
- [ICO research-processing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/principles-and-grounds-for-processing/): there is **no automatic UK GDPR lawful basis called “research”**. The ICO notes its guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act; a qualified controller must assess the actual flow under current law.

This pack deliberately does not choose legal jurisdiction outside the current E&W *comparison* nor imply any ability to operate overseas merely because UK registration might eventually occur. A charity lawyer/accountant must reassess applicable law at the time of a real action.
