# Strategic operating model — Global Prosperity Platform V0.1

**Status:** candidate, 16 November 2026, developed under founder authorisation for the HPI-SCALE transformation. Founder authorisation to develop a strategy is **not** independent scientific, community or legal approval. Supersedes nothing in the [founding charter](../charter.en.md); extends the [operating model V0.3](../OPERATING_MODEL.md).

## Why change the operating model

The three early cases — Pension Credit in Newham, CadÚnico/Bolsa Família in Recife and Assegno di Inclusione in Naples — were feasibility studies, not interventions. Repeating "one city, one problem, one tool" would generate worthwhile research but little leverage. The same underlying mechanisms (information and learning costs, compliance burden, eligibility uncertainty, procedural denials) appear in all three programme pathways even though rules, institutions and data differ. HPI therefore treats **mechanisms, methods and evaluation designs** as its reusable research objects.

**Operating philosophy:** universal ambition, focused execution, verifiable impact. HPI's importance should depend on the independently verified improvement it enables others to achieve, not on the number of projects it runs.

Supporting workstreams: **HPI-AI** (AI research assistance and evidence reliability) and **HPI-QA** (integrated quality, security and release verification). The umbrella initiative is **HPI-SCALE**. These are logical workstreams, not separate organisations, repositories or databases.

## Strategic horizons

- **Horizon A — evidence into practice (0–5 years, indicative):** practical, evidence-based mechanisms for reducing poverty and barriers to prosperity, evaluated by qualified partners.
- **Horizon B — understanding the systems (5–20+ years, research begins now):** the Economic Systems Lab, starting with bounded, testable questions. Horizon B informs Horizon A from the start; neither presumes an economic doctrine.

## 12–24 month horizon (conditional)

1. **Coverage:** add jurisdictions only where official sources, rights and comparability are verified; prioritise full-text extraction and retraction checks for existing evidence.
2. **Scientific review:** commission independent review of the knowledge-base methods, the benefit-access package and the Systems Lab model (G-05).
3. **Evaluation partnerships:** only with accountable institutions that hold lawful data access and ethics approval; HPI contributes methods, not unauthorised fieldwork.
4. **Adoption:** record adoption only with public evidence verified independently of HPI.
5. **Systems research:** calibrate the household model to official survey microdata through a qualified partner; register and test competing explanations.
6. **AI:** activate a local or approved provider for claim-extraction drafts, with human review and published error rates before any expansion.

No delivery date in this section is a commitment.

## Impact measurement principles

Separate technical reach, research usefulness, adoption and demonstrated human benefit; never substitute applications for receipt or receipt for sustained improvement; publish null, negative and adverse findings; report distributional effects and costs; state missingness and uncertainty explicitly.

## Governance and decision boundaries

| Decision | Who may decide today | Not sufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Software, documentation and desk research | Founder custodian via reviewed pull requests | — |
| Scientific validity of methods or claims | Independent qualified reviewers (G-05) | Founder, AI, CI checks |
| Fieldwork, recruitment, personal data | Accountable legal operator plus independent ethics (G-02, G-04) | Any HPI document or website state |
| Partnerships and adoption claims | Verified public evidence from the organisation | Developer judgment |
| Charter changes | Open review and attributable ratification (F0-01 #10) | This document |

## Charter implications

The four-pillar model changes **how** HPI pursues its mission, not the mission, values, two horizons or safeguards in the charter. A **candidate addendum** is therefore proposed rather than a charter edit: *"HPI pursues its mission primarily by developing reusable, openly reviewable knowledge, methods and software that address recurring mechanisms across contexts, and by enabling qualified institutions to evaluate and adapt them."* It is registered as an open item in the [charter review register](../charter-review-register-v0.4.md) under F0-01 (#10) and is **not ratified**.
